DOCUMENT 04: OFFICIAL FEE STRUCTURE & PAYMENT REGULATIONS (2026-2027)

1. INTERIM / APPROVED TUITION & DEVELOPMENT FEES (PER ANNUM)
The fee structure for B.E. programs at Sinhgad Academy of Engineering is determined and approved by the Fees Regulating Authority (FRA), Maharashtra State.

Fee Breakdown for Academic Year 2026-2027 (Undergraduate B.E.):
- Open / General Category (Total Fee):
  * Tuition Fee: Rs. 84,348
  * Development Fee: Rs. 10,652
  * University / Examination / Gymkhana Charges: Rs. 3,500
  * Refundable Caution Money Deposit (One-time): Rs. 2,000
  * Total Payable at Admission: Rs. 100,500 per annum

- Category-Wise Concessional Fee Structure (Under Govt. of Maharashtra Welfare Schemes):
  * OBC / EBC (Economically Backward Class) / SEBC Candidates:
    - 50% Tuition Fee Concession under Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna.
    - Total Payable at Admission: Rs. 58,324 per annum.
  * VJ / NT / SBC / TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) Candidates:
    - 100% Tuition Fee Concession (Only Development Fee + Other University charges payable).
    - Total Payable at Admission: Rs. 16,152 per annum.
  * SC / ST Candidates:
    - 100% Tuition & Development Fee Exemption (Only University & Eligibility charges payable).
    - Total Payable at Admission: Rs. 5,500 per annum.
