2. SPPU CHOICE BASED CREDIT SYSTEM (CBCS)
- The curriculum follows Savitribai Phule Pune University's Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) pattern.
- Total Credits Required for B.E. Degree: 160 Credits distributed across 8 semesters.
- Evaluation Scheme per Course:
  * In-Semester Examination (In-Sem): 30 Marks conducted mid-semester by SPPU.
  * End-Semester Examination (End-Sem): 70 Marks conducted at semester conclusion by SPPU.
  * Term Work (TW) / Continuous Assessment: 25 to 50 Marks evaluated based on timely lab assignment submissions, attendance, and mini-projects.
  * Practical / Oral Examination (PR/OR): 25 to 50 Marks assessed jointly by internal and external university examiners.
- Passing Standard: Minimum 40% aggregate in End-Sem and In-Sem combined, with at least 28 marks out of 70 in the End-Sem written exam.

3. ACADEMIC CALENDAR & SCHEDULE
- Academic Year Structure: Divided into two terms (Term I: July to November; Term II: January to May).
- In-Sem Examinations: Typically scheduled in the 6th to 8th week of the semester.
- Term-Work Submission & Mock Oral Examinations: Conducted 2 weeks prior to university theory examinations.
- Remedial & Bridge Courses: Conducted during the first 3 weeks for Direct Second Year (DSE) lateral entry students to cover foundational concepts.

4. ATTENDANCE MONITORING & ERP SYSTEM
- Minimum 75% attendance in theory lectures and 100% attendance in practical laboratory sessions is strictly mandatory as per Savitribai Phule Pune University Ordinance 0.60.
- Daily attendance is logged through the STES Central GEMS ERP portal (gems.sinhgad.edu).
- Attendance Defaulters List: Published on the 5th of every month. Students with attendance between 60% and 75% on genuine medical grounds must submit medical certificates attested by the campus health center. Students below 60% are detained from appearing in university semester examinations.

5. ACADEMIC DEAN & COUNSELING CELL
- Academic Monitoring Committee: Headed by Vice-Principal & Academic Coordinator, SAOE.
- Teacher-Guardian Scheme (TGS): Every student is assigned a dedicated faculty counselor (TG) for continuous academic tracking, career guidance, and parent-teacher communication.
