DOCUMENT 03: ACADEMIC DEPARTMENTS, SPECIALIZED LABORATORIES & FACULTY (2026-2027)

1. DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER ENGINEERING
- Head of Department (HOD): Dr. S. B. Shinde (Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering).
- Overview: State-of-the-art computational infrastructure with over 350 high-end desktop workstations running Ubuntu Linux and Windows Enterprise with gigabit internet connectivity.
- Specialized Laboratories:
  * Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Laboratory (NVIDIA GPU workstations).
  * Data Science & Cloud Computing Lab (AWS Academy & Google Cloud certified environment).
  * Cyber Security & Digital Forensics Lab.
  * Open Source Software & Advanced Programming Lab.
- Student Association: ACES (Association of Computer Engineering Students) organizes annual hackathons, code sprints, and technical paper presentations.
- Department Contact: hodcomp.saoe@sinhgad.edu | Ext: 210.

2. DEPARTMENT OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
- Head of Department (HOD): Prof. A. B. Bagwan (M.E., Ph.D. Pursuing in Information Technology).
- Overview: Focuses on full-stack web architectures, mobile application development, database management systems, and DevOps methodologies.
- Specialized Laboratories:
  * Mobile Computing & Full-Stack Development Lab.
  * Database & Big Data Analytics Lab.
  * Human-Computer Interaction & Software Engineering Lab.
- Student Association: ITSA (Information Technology Students Association) arranges expert guest lectures from Pune IT industry leaders, open-source workshops, and coding bootcamps.
- Department Contact: hodit.saoe@sinhgad.edu | Ext: 220.

- Head of Department (HOD): Dr. K. P. Patil (Ph.D. in Signal Processing and Wireless Communication).

- Specialized Laboratories:
  * VLSI Design & Embedded Systems Lab (Cadence, Xilinx Vivado software suite).

  * Digital Signal & Image Processing Lab.
- Student Association: ETSA (Electronics & Telecommunication Students Association) organizes Roborace, IoT Project Competitions, and Circuit Design Contests.
- Department Contact: hodentc.saoe@sinhgad.edu | Ext: 230.

- Head of Department (HOD): Dr. S. P. Patil (Ph.D. in Thermal Engineering).

  * Central Workshop (Lathe, Milling, Shaper, Foundry, Welding and Carpentry sections).
