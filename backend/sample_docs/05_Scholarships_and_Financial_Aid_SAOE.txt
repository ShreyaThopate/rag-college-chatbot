DOCUMENT 05: SCHOLARSHIPS, GOVERNMENT CONCESSIONS & FINANCIAL AID (2026-2027)

1. GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA SCHOLARSHIPS (MahaDBT PORTAL)
Sinhgad Academy of Engineering facilitates all Central and Maharashtra State Government scholarship and freeship schemes through the MahaDBT Portal (mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in).

Major Available Schemes:
- Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC Scheme):
  * Eligibility: Open category students admitted through CAP rounds whose family annual gross income is less than or equal to Rs. 8,00,000.
  * Benefit: 50% waiver in Tuition Fee and 50% waiver in Exam Fee.
  * Requirement: Income Certificate from Tahsildar, Domicile Certificate, Ration Card, and minimum 75% attendance.

- Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Hostel Maintenance Allowance (Hostel Nirvah Bhatta):
  * Eligibility: Meritorious students from rural agricultural / marginal labor families admitted through CAP whose family annual income is up to Rs. 8,00,000 staying in college hostel or private rented accommodation in Pune.
  * Benefit: Rs. 30,000 per academic year for Pune MMR region.

- Post-Matric Scholarship for Backward Classes (SC / ST / VJNT / NT / SBC / OBC):
  * Social Justice and Special Assistance Department & Tribal Development Department schemes.
  * Benefits: 100% Tuition Fee waiver for SC/ST and 100% Tuition Fee waiver for VJNT/SBC/NT (below income limits). 50% Tuition Fee for OBC (family income up to Rs. 8 Lakhs).
  * Mandatory: Caste Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, Non-Creamy Layer (where applicable), and Nationalized Bank Account linked with Aadhaar NPCI.

2. CENTRAL GOVERNMENT & MINORITY SCHOLARSHIPS (NSP PORTAL)
- National Scholarship Portal (NSP - scholarships.gov.in):
  * Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for Professional and Technical Courses CS (Minority Communities: Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi).
  * Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships for College and University Students (MHRD/DoHE) for top 20th percentile scorers in Std. XII board exams.
  * AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls: Rs. 50,000 per year for female engineering students admitted via CAP.
  * AICTE Saksham Scholarship: Rs. 50,000 per year for specially-abled students with disability not less than 40%.

3. SINHGAD TECHNICAL EDUCATION SOCIETY (STES) INSTITUTIONAL AID
- Sinhgad Student Welfare Fund: Special financial assistance and contingency grants provided to students who encounter unforeseen parental bereavement or medical emergencies during their course of study.
- Smt. Kashibai Navale Academic Excellence Award: Annual cash prizes and citations awarded to university rank holders and departmental toppers.
- Sports & Cultural Achievement Awards: Full or partial sponsorship for students representing Sinhgad Academy of Engineering at inter-university, state, and national sports tournaments.

4. SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION WORKFLOW & CRITICAL DEADLINES
- Application Period: Opens in August upon college admission; first cycle closes on October 31, 2026; renewal verification on MahaDBT continues until December 15, 2026.
- In-House Verification: The College Student Scholarship Section verifies all uploaded documents on the MahaDBT system within 7 working days of submission.
- Aadhaar-Bank Seeding Requirement: Students must ensure their bank account is seeded with Aadhaar and NPCI mapped to receive DBT disbursements directly.
