DOCUMENT 01: ADMISSIONS GUIDELINES & SEAT ALLOCATION (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

1. INSTITUTIONAL OVERVIEW & AFFILIATION
Sinhgad Academy of Engineering (SAOE) is a premier technical institute established in 2005 under the aegis of Sinhgad Technical Education Society (STES), founded by Prof. M. N. Navale. The institute operates on a sprawling lush-green campus at Kondhwa (Bk), Pune - 411048. SAOE offers undergraduate Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programs approved by AICTE and affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU). The Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell (State CET Cell) / Directorate of Technical Education (DTE) Choice Code for Sinhgad Academy of Engineering is 6187.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR FIRST YEAR B.E. ADMISSIONS
- Maharashtra State Candidature Candidates:
  * Candidate must be an Indian National.
  * Passed HSC (Std. XII) examination of Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education or its equivalent examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one of the subjects: Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices, Agriculture, Engineering Graphics, or Business Studies.
  * Obtained at least 45% aggregate marks (at least 40% aggregate marks in case of Backward Class categories, Economically Weaker Section (EWS), and Persons with Disability candidates belonging to Maharashtra State only) in the above subjects taken together.
  * Obtained non-zero positive score in MHT-CET 2026 conducted by the State CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra.

- All India Candidature Candidates (AI Quota / JEE Main):
  * Candidate must satisfy the basic educational qualifications as above.
  * Obtained non-zero positive score in JEE (Main) 2026 Paper-I or MHT-CET 2026. Preference is given to JEE (Main) scores over MHT-CET for All India seats.

- Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE) Admissions (Lateral Entry):
  * Passed Diploma in Engineering and Technology with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories of Maharashtra State) from an AICTE-approved institution.
  * OR Passed B.Sc. Degree from a recognized university with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories) and passed 10+2 with Mathematics.

3. CENTRALIZED ADMISSION PROCESS (CAP) & COUNSELING
- Admissions to 80% CAP seats and 20% Institute Level / Minority Quota seats are conducted strictly as per the rules and directives published by the State CET Cell, Maharashtra State (cetcell.mahacet.org).
- DTE Institute Code: 6187.
- Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS): 5% additional seats over and above the sanctioned intake are allotted via CAP rounds to meritorious students whose parents' annual gross family income is less than Rs. 8,00,000.
- Economically Weaker Sections (EWS): 10% supernumerary seats are allocated as per Government of Maharashtra norms.

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR ADMISSION VERIFICATION
Applicants must present the following original documents along with three sets of self-attested photocopies:
1. MHT-CET 2026 Score Card / JEE (Main) 2026 Rank Card.
2. CAP Seat Allotment Letter issued by State CET Cell.
3. SSC (Std. X) Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth).
4. HSC (Std. XII) Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate.
5. Diploma / B.Sc. Final Year Mark Sheet (for Direct Second Year Admissions).
6. School / College Leaving Certificate (Transfer Certificate - TC).
7. Indian Nationality Certificate and Domicile Certificate of Maharashtra State.
8. Caste Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, and Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (valid up to March 31, 2027) for OBC, VJ/DT-NT, SBC candidates.
9. Income Certificate issued by competent revenue authority (Tahsil Office) for EBC / TFWS / Scholarship claims.
10. Aadhaar Card copy (mandatory for biometric authentication and scholarship registration).
11. Four recent passport-size colored photographs.
12. Migration Certificate (for candidates from boards/universities outside Maharashtra).
13. Anti-Ragging Affidavit signed by the student and parent (online registered at antiragging.in).
