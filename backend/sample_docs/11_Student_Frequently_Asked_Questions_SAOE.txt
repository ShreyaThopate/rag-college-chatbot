DOCUMENT 11: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (STUDENT FAQs) (2026-2027)

Q1: What is the official DTE Choice Code and Institute Code for Sinhgad Academy of Engineering?
A: The DTE Institute Code for Sinhgad Academy of Engineering is EN6187. The branch-specific DTE Choice Codes are:
- Computer Engineering: 618724510
- Information Technology: 618724610
- Electronics & Telecommunication (E&TC): 618737210
- Mechanical Engineering: 618761210
- Civil Engineering: 618719110

Q2: Which university is Sinhgad Academy of Engineering affiliated with?
A: Sinhgad Academy of Engineering is permanently affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), formerly the University of Pune. All undergraduate B.E. and postgraduate M.E. degrees are conferred directly by SPPU.

Q3: How can a student obtain a Bonafide Certificate or Transcript?
A: Students requiring a Bonafide Certificate (for passport, bank education loan, bus pass, or visa processing) must submit a prescribed requisition slip along with a copy of their college ID card and latest fee receipt to the Student Section (Room 104, Ground Floor). Bonafide certificates are processed and issued within 24 to 48 working hours.

Q4: What is the college attendance policy and defaulter threshold?
A: In accordance with SPPU Ordinance 0.60, maintaining at least 75% attendance in theory lectures and practical laboratories individually is strictly compulsory. Students with attendance falling below 75% without authorized medical grounds are listed on the Monthly Defaulter List and can be detained from appearing in SPPU semester examinations.

Q5: Is bus transport facility available for day scholars attending SAOE Kondhwa?
A: Yes, Sinhgad Technical Education Society operates a dedicated fleet of college buses connecting the Kondhwa campus with key Pune locations including Swargate, Katraj, Hadapsar, Fatima Nagar, Kothrud, Camp, and Pune Railway Station. Students can register for bus transport at the Student Section.

Q6: How do EBC and category students apply for fee concession and scholarships?
A: Eligible Open category students with family income <= Rs. 8 Lakhs can apply for the 50% tuition fee waiver under the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC) via the government MahaDBT portal (mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in). Reserved category students (OBC, VJNT, SC, ST, SBC) must apply on the same portal before the government deadline and submit verified hard copies to Room 105.

Q7: What is the procedure for obtaining a duplicate College Identity Card if lost?
A: The student must lodge an online lost property report (NCR) with Pune Police, submit an application stating the reason to the Office Superintendent (Room 104), pay the duplicate card fee of Rs. 200 at the Accounts Counter, and obtain the new RFID identity card from the IT department.

Q8: How are campus placements conducted at Sinhgad Academy of Engineering?
A: Placements are conducted centrally by the Sinhgad Central Placement Cell (CPC) in coordination with SAOE's Departmental T&P team. Over 300+ recruiters including TCS, Accenture, Cognizant, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, Persistent, and Core Engineering firms conduct on-campus and pooled drives for eligible final year students.

Q9: What are the central library working hours and book borrowing limits?
A: The Central Library is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on regular working days and remains open 24/7 during examination periods. Undergraduate students can borrow up to 3 books for 14 days, renewable once online.

Q10: Who should a student contact in case of an emergency on campus or in the hostel?
A: In case of medical emergencies, visit the Campus Medical Health Center or call the 24/7 helpline at 020-26934560 / +91-9881009900. For hostel assistance, contact the Chief Rector at +91-9881004455 or the Main Security Gate at 020-26934559.
