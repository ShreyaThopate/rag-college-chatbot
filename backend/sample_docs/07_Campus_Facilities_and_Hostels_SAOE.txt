DOCUMENT 07: CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE, HOSTELS & STUDENT FACILITIES (2026-2027)

1. CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE & SURROUNDINGS
The Sinhgad Academy of Engineering campus is located in the scenic hills of Kondhwa (Bk), Pune - 411048. The campus spans over multi-acre landscaped grounds featuring modern multi-storeyed academic buildings, seminar halls, an open-air amphitheater, and high-speed campus-wide optical fiber Wi-Fi.

2. CENTRAL LIBRARY & DIGITAL COMMONS
- Collection & Space: Spacious 2-floor library accommodating over 35,000+ print volumes, 8,500+ unique titles, standard reference handbooks, and subscription to 75+ national and international print journals.
- Digital Library: 40 dedicated high-performance terminals providing access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, ASCE, ASME, SpringerLink, and DELNET digital databases.
- Reading Hall: Air-conditioned reading hall with seating capacity for 450+ students.
- Library Working Hours:
  * Regular Days: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
  * During SPPU In-Sem and End-Sem Examination Periods: Open 24/7.
- Book Bank Scheme: Full semester textbook sets are provided to SC/ST and needy meritorious students free of cost.
- Circulation Rules: Students can issue up to 3 books simultaneously for a loan duration of 14 calendar days (renewable once online).

3. RESIDENTIAL HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION
- Dedicated on-campus hostel buildings:
  * Sahyadri & Shivneri Halls (Boys' Hostels - 600+ capacity).
  * Saraswati & Savitribai Halls (Girls' Hostels - 500+ capacity).
- Room Configuration: Clean, well-ventilated 3-seater and 4-seater rooms furnished with individual steel beds, study desks, book racks, and cupboards.
- Amenities: 24-hour hot water powered by solar heating panels, uninterrupted power backup with diesel generators, purified RO drinking water dispensers on all floors, and automatic laundry facilities.
- Hostel Timings & Curfew:
  * Entry Curfew: 9:30 PM on weekdays; 10:00 PM on weekends with prior permission.
  * Biometric attendance punch is mandatory at the hostel entrance every evening.

4. DINING, CAFETERIA & RECREATION
- Central Mess Facility: Serves hygienic, balanced vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals 4 times a day (Breakfast: 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM; Lunch: 12:15 PM - 2:00 PM; Tea/Snacks: 5:00 PM - 6:15 PM; Dinner: 7:30 PM - 9:30 PM).
- Campus Cafeteria: Multi-cuisine food court serving snacks, fresh fruit juices, and beverages until 10:30 PM.
- Sports & Gymnasium: Multi-purpose outdoor playground for cricket, football, and athletics; dedicated basketball and volleyball courts; indoor badminton courts and table tennis; separate fully-equipped gymnasium for boys and girls with trained fitness instructors.

5. HEALTHCARE & BANKING SERVICES
- Campus Medical Health Center: Resident doctor and full-time nurse available for first-aid and routine health consultations; 24/7 dedicated emergency ambulance stationed on campus for transfer to Smt. Kashibai Navale General Hospital, Narhe/Ambegaon.
- Banking & ATM: Canara Bank and Bank of Maharashtra on-campus full-service branch with 24-hour ATM kiosks.
- Campus Transport: STES operates dedicated private bus routes connecting Sinhgad Kondhwa campus with Swargate, Katraj, Hadapsar, Kothrud, Nigdi, and Pune Railway Station.
