DOCUMENT 08: INSTITUTIONAL CODE OF CONDUCT, RULES & STATUTORY POLICIES (2026-2027)

1. MANDATORY CAMPUS DISCIPLINE & DRESS CODE
- Identity Card Mandate: Every student must wear their official RFID-enabled Sinhgad Identity Card with lanyard prominently around their neck while inside the academic building, laboratories, library, and examination halls.
- Dress Code & Decorum: Students are expected to maintain professional and dignified formal/semi-formal attire during regular college hours. During laboratory sessions and workshop hours, protective safety gear (aprons, safety goggles, and closed-toe leather shoes) is strictly mandatory.
- Use of Mobile Phones: Use of mobile phones during classroom lectures, tutorial sessions, and practical laboratories is strictly prohibited and subject to confiscation.

2. ZERO TOLERANCE ANTI-RAGGING POLICY
- In accordance with the Supreme Court of India directives and UGC / AICTE regulations (Prevention and Prohibition of Ragging in Technical Institutions), Sinhgad Academy of Engineering maintains a strict ZERO-TOLERANCE policy against ragging.
- Ragging is a cognizable criminal offense punishable under the Maharashtra Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1999.
- Actions against guilty individuals include: Immediate suspension from college, expulsion from hostel, rustication from university, lodging of First Information Report (FIR) with Pune Police, and debarment from taking admission in any other technical institution for 5 years.
- Anti-Ragging Committee Chairman: Principal, SAOE | Mobile: +91-9881001122.
- 24/7 National Anti-Ragging Helpline (Toll-Free): 1800-180-5522 | Email: helpline@antiragging.in.

3. ATTENDANCE & EXAMINATION REGULATIONS
- Minimum 75% Attendance: Compulsory across all theoretical subjects and practical laboratories individually in each semester.
- Medical Leave Exemption: Genuine illness leave requests must be submitted along with medical fitness certificates within 3 working days of resumption of classes to the Department Academic Coordinator.
- In-Sem and End-Sem Rules: Students must report to the examination hall 20 minutes prior to the scheduled examination time. Possession of electronic gadgets, programmable calculators, or handwritten chits constitutes an offense under SPPU Malpractice Statutes (Section 32/5).

4. STATUTORY COMMITTEES & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL CELLS
- Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) / POSH Cell: Conformed as per Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 to ensure a secure and supportive environment for female students and staff members. Presiding Officer: Dr. M. S. Gaikwad | Email: icc.saoe@sinhgad.edu.
- SC / ST / OBC Grievance Cell: Redresses any form of caste-based discrimination and oversees welfare scholarship processing. In-Charge: Prof. R. B. Kakkeri.
- College Grievance Redressal Cell (CGRC): Online portal for filing student grievances regarding academic evaluation, infrastructure, or fee receipts at saoe.sinhgad.edu/grievance.
- Anti-Drug & Substance Abuse Committee: Strict prohibition of smoking, consumption of alcohol, or possession of any banned substances on campus grounds. Violators face immediate police handover and institutional expulsion.
